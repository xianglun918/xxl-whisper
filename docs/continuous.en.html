Pipeline

Five-step pipeline

From microphone to cursor, everything runs locally — no network round-trip.

STEP 1

Microphone

16 kHz always-on capture, no key to press.

→
STEP 2

512-sample chunking

Split into frames of 512 samples and feed the VAD frame by frame.

→
STEP 3

VAD gate

Silero decides whether it is speech; silence longer than min_silence ends the sentence.

→
STEP 4

Offline recognition

A local model decodes only that speech; works offline.

→
STEP 5

On screen

The result is injected at the current cursor.

The catch: how you feed it decides everything

The same 8.9s recording, fed two different ways — a 23× difference in result.

Whole clip at once

✗

Tuning

Four knobs

The defaults suit most cases; drag a slider to see the range.

vad_thresholdDefault 0.5
00.51

Higher is more conservative: the gate opens only for clearer speech, with fewer false triggers.

vad_min_speech_msDefault 250
0250ms1000ms

Bursts shorter than this are dropped, so clicks never become text.

vad_min_silence_msDefault 500
100ms500ms2000ms

How much silence ends a sentence; smaller means faster, choppier segmentation.

vad_max_speech_msDefault 20000
2s20s60s

Forces very long speech to split, so a single sentence can't grow without bound.

Get started

How to turn it on

One checkbox in the tray menu; the app handles the rest.

Tray menu
  • Continuous dictation
  • Model▸
  • Hotkey▸
  • Launch at startup
  • Quit
  1. 1
    Check "Continuous dictation"

    Turn the switch on in the tray right-click menu.

  2. 2
    VAD downloads automatically

    On first enable it downloads the ~1.8MB silero_vad.onnx; offline-ready afterwards.

  3. 3
    Just speak

    No key to press; finish a sentence and the text appears at the cursor.

  4. 4
    Click again to turn off

    Unchecking stops it; the mode is remembered and reused next launch.
